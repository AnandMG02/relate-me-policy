# Relate Me Child Safety Standards

**Effective date: October 25, 2026**

These Child Safety Standards apply to **Relate Me** and everyone who uses it. Relate Me prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. These standards are publicly available so users can understand what is prohibited and how to report a concern.

## Prohibited conduct and content

Users must not use Relate Me to:

- Create, upload, store, request, share, promote, or distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including sexual images or videos of anyone under 18.
- Groom, solicit, sexualize, exploit, traffic, sextort, threaten, or otherwise sexually abuse or endanger a child.
- Encourage, facilitate, or help another person engage in any of this conduct.
- Use family profiles, photos, messages, invitations, or other app features to contact or exploit a child for a sexual purpose.

This prohibition applies whether the conduct is direct, attempted, or facilitated through another person or service.

## Reporting a concern

Use the **Child Safety & Reporting** feature in Relate Me to report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation, inappropriate content, or another safety issue. The feature prepares a report in your email app addressed to **innvoice.naachyss@gmail.com**. Review it and choose **Send** to submit it. If you cannot use the in-app feature, email that address with the subject “Relate Me child safety report.”

Include a description and, if known, the relevant account or profile. **Do not attach, download, forward, or otherwise share suspected illegal images or videos.** If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

## How reports are handled

Relate Me will review safety reports and take appropriate action consistent with these standards and applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, action may include removing content, restricting or terminating an account, preserving relevant information as permitted or required by law, and cooperating with law enforcement or child-protection authorities.

Relate Me prohibits CSAM and will take appropriate action when it becomes aware of it. Confirmed CSAM will be handled and reported to the appropriate authority when required by applicable law. Users must not redistribute suspected CSAM as a way to report it.
